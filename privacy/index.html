Vocablora

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Türkçe

Your decks and settings stay on your device. The Vocablora app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies, and it makes no internet connection of its own.

1. Who we are

Vocablora is a vocabulary flashcard app made by Arda Yıldız, who is the data controller for it. You can reach us at app.ardayildiz@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Vocablora app distributed through the App Store and Google Play.

2. What stays on your device

Vocablora stores the following in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.

  • Your decks: their names, languages, level, words, meanings and examples, and when they were created and changed.
  • Your settings: the app language, theme, deck layout, study method and your learning and meaning languages.
  • An unfinished deck you are adding, so it is not lost while you switch to a chat app. It is removed when you save or discard it.

Vocablora does not save your study sessions or results. The app reads your device's language list to choose the app language, but does not store that list.

Depending on your device and its backup settings, your device's own backup, such as iCloud or a Google backup, can include the app's data. That backup belongs to your account with Apple or Google, and we cannot see it.

3. No internet connection of its own

The app contains everything it needs. It makes no internet connection of its own and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. The only exceptions are the ones you start yourself: opening these pages from Settings or the Licences page (section 11) and writing to us (section 7). It has no update check of its own: new versions come through the App Store or Google Play.

4. The App Store and Google Play

Apple and Google handle downloading, installing and updating the app, under their own privacy policies. If you allow your device to share diagnostics with app developers, they may give us crash and performance reports, such as the device model, the system version and where the app failed. These reports do not contain your decks, and we use them only to find and fix problems.

5. Making decks with a chat service

To add a deck, you can copy a prompt and paste it into a chat service you choose. The prompt contains only your chosen languages, the level and fixed instructions. Vocablora sends nothing to the chat service and never reads your clipboard: you copy and paste yourself. What you type into that service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications.

7. If you e-mail us

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address, any name in it and your message. We use them only to answer you. We delete the conversation 12 months after its last message. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete a deck at any time in the app.
  • To delete everything, delete the app: this removes its data from your device. A device backup made earlier keeps its copy until that backup is replaced or deleted.
  • Vocablora keeps no copy of your decks and cannot restore lost decks.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because your decks never reach us, the only personal data we process is the e-mail you send us and any crash reports described in section 4, which we view in Apple's and Google's developer tools and do not keep elsewhere. To use your rights, write to us. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

Vocablora is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not ask for age or date of birth and hold no age data.

10. Transfers abroad

Google, which provides our mailbox, processes e-mails in the United States. Apple and Google process the data described in section 4 in several countries, including the United States, under their own policies. For users in Türkiye, the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni gives the details.

11. These pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical data of anyone who opens them in a browser, such as the IP address, under its own privacy statement.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. A change to what data leaves your device is described here before the app version that makes it is released.

13. Languages

This policy is also available in Türkçe. If the versions differ, the English version applies, except where the law requires the Turkish version. For users in Türkiye, the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni gives the information the KVKK requires.
