Add a deck

  1. Open Add. Choose the language you are learning, the language of the meanings and your level, then tap Continue.
  2. Tap Copy prompt and paste it into a chat service of your choice, together with the words you want to learn. The chat answers with your cards in a short block of text called JSON.
  3. Back in Vocablora, tap I have the JSON, paste the answer, give the deck a name and tap Save deck.

Each card in the JSON looks like this; the example sentence is optional:

[
  {
    "term": "apple",
    "meaning": "elma",
    "example": {
      "sentence": "I eat an apple.",
      "translation": "Bir elma yerim."
    }
  }
]

If something is wrong with the pasted text, Vocablora says what it could not read and keeps your text, so you can fix it. An unfinished deck is kept while you switch to the chat and back.

Study

Open a deck and choose how to study:

  • Browse: flip through every word at your own pace. Tap the card, or press Enter or Space on a keyboard, to flip it. Swipe, or use the arrow keys, to move between cards.
  • Learn: sort the words into Know and Still learning. Swipe right if you know a word and left if not (the other way round in right-to-left languages such as Arabic), or use the buttons or the arrow keys. Undo takes back the last answer. After a round you can study the words you are still learning again.

Every session starts with all the words of the deck, in order or shuffled. Vocablora does not keep scores or history between sessions.

Settings

In Settings you can choose the app language, the theme (system, light or dark), how decks are laid out, and your usual learning and meaning languages.

Your data

  • Your decks and settings are stored in the app on this device. There is no account and no cloud copy of our own, so decks do not sync between devices.
  • To delete a deck, open the deck menu and choose Delete.
  • Deleting the app deletes your decks, unless your device's own backup brings them back. Vocablora cannot restore them itself.

More in the privacy policy.

Something is not working

  • If the app does not open or behaves oddly, close it completely and open it again.
  • If Vocablora says it cannot save, check that your device has free storage space, then tap Reload saved data.
